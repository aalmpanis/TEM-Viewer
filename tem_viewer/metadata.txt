about=TEM-Viewer loads 1D inversion models of TEM / AEM soundings from .xyz files (plain column files or files with a '/'-commented header; the coordinate system is read from the header when it is there) as point layers, and works on any sounding layers you tick.
 TEM data: import .xyz files, list the sounding layers and colour soundings on the map by any attribute, with a colour bar on the map.
 Profile: resistivity sections along a line drawn on the map, through soundings selected on the map, along a line from a line layer or along a saved profile; bars or interpolated, DOI mask, data misfit, direction on the map, cursor linked to the map, adjustable colour scales, optional topography from a DEM and boreholes from a borehole layer. For IP inversions the phase, tau, C, chargeability (or any other per-layer model parameter in the file) can be plotted too, stacked in panels with their own colour scales. Profiles can be saved (GeoPackage) and loaded again for editing.
 Mean maps: mean resistivity (or IP parameter) in depth or elevation slices, interpolated with inverse distance or ordinary kriging, added to the map as GeoTIFF layers.
 Geological Model: pick the surfaces between formations on a section and plot the geological section; surfaces can be exported as 3D lines.
 Optional Python packages: scipy (faster neighbour search, variogram fitting), openpyxl (Excel files).
